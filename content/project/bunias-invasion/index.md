My doctoral research (Department of Chemical Ecology, Bielefeld University, 2019–2023) investigated why some populations of *Bunias orientalis* (Warty cabbage) become invasive in Central Europe. I compared plants from naturalised and invasive populations in terms of growth, competitive ability and functional traits, and studied their responses to fungal infection and insect herbivory under different nutrient supplies.
