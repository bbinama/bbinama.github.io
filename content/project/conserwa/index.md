As a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Crop Sciences (Crop Genetics), University of Göttingen, I study how environmental variables shape evidence-based support for transitioning to agroecological weed management in diverse farming systems and European regions, as part of the [CONSERWA](https://conserwa.eu/) project.
