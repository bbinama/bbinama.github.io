Gishwati-Mukura in western Rwanda is a landscape where degraded montane forests are being restored. Invasive trees can undermine these restoration efforts.

This project, supported by the **UNESCO Man and the Biosphere (MAB) Young Scientist Research Award 2024**, investigates how to understand and manage invasive plants in forest restoration. Using ensemble species distribution models, we identify where invasive trees are most likely to spread so that control efforts can be prioritised.

**Related manuscript:** Binama, B., Ntawubizigira, E., & Tuyisabe, A. *Evidence-based management of invasive trees: Using ensemble species distribution models to prioritise control in Rwandan restored montane forests.* Submitted to *Ecological Solutions and Evidence*.
