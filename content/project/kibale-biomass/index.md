Kibale National Park in western Uganda is one of East Africa's most important tropical forests, home to a high diversity of trees and primates. Accurate estimates of how much carbon these forests store are essential for conservation planning and climate policy.

This project, funded by the **GEO-TREES 2025 Research Awards Program**, integrates multi-scale remote sensing with ecological field data to improve biomass estimation in Kibale.
